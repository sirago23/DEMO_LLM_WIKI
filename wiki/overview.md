# Overview

This wiki is the FAQ knowledge base for **Seattle Coffee Gear** — a US-based online retailer of coffee equipment and coffee products.

---

## Domain

Covers customer-facing policies and product information for Seattle Coffee Gear, including shipping, ordering, cancellations, claims, and product-specific rules. Intended to power a LINE FAQ chatbot that answers customer questions grounded entirely in this wiki.

## Key themes

- **Shipping** — Free shipping threshold ($49+), paid shipping cutoffs, carrier rules, geographic limitations (US only)
- **Order management** — Strict no-change policy after tracking; Beanz™ subscription orders lock even earlier
- **Claims and issues** — 5-business-day window to report damaged, missing, or defective items
- **Special product lines** — Beanz™ subscription coffee (stricter rules); Open Box equipment (Final Sale)
- **Shipping protection** — Safonia add-on for loss/theft/damage coverage

## Open questions / gaps

- Return policy (RMA process) — referenced but not yet ingested
- Refund policy details beyond the refused-delivery penalty
- Product catalog (plans, machine categories, price ranges)
- Contact details and customer support hours
- International shipping alternatives (if any)
