# Beanz™ (Powered by Beanz)

Seattle Coffee Gear's coffee subscription and fulfillment service, indicated by the "Powered by Beanz™" logo on applicable product pages.

## Key facts

- Applies to most coffees not listed under "European Favorites"
- Identified by the "Powered by Beanz™" logo under "Product details" on the product page
- Includes recurring subscription orders

## Special shipping and order rules

- **Orders containing Beanz™ items cannot be changed or cancelled as soon as the order is placed** — this is stricter than the general SCG policy (which locks at tracking generation)
- Address changes are not possible for the Beanz™ portion of an order
- A specific shipping method cannot be selected for Beanz™ shipments; contact SCG before ordering if a specific method is required
- Recurring subscription orders follow the same immediate-lock rule
