# Safonia Shipping Protection

An optional add-on purchased at checkout that protects orders from loss, theft, and damage during transit.

## Key facts

- Available as an optional add-on at checkout
- Covers the period from checkout through delivery
- Protects against: loss, theft, and damage
- Offers an easy claims process

## How to use

If your package is lost, stolen, or arrives damaged:
1. Keep your order details
2. Take photos of any damage if applicable
3. File a claim — see the SCG shipping protection FAQ page for the claims process
