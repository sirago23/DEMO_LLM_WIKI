# Source: Shipping Policy — Seattle Coffee Gear

**Original:** `raw/Shipping policy _ Seattle Coffee Gear.pdf` | **Ingested:** 2026-08-07

## Summary

Full shipping policy page for Seattle Coffee Gear, covering geographic coverage, free and paid shipping terms, order cancellation and change rules, address change restrictions, refused delivery fees, damaged/missing merchandise reporting, open box equipment rules, commercial freight, tax and payment methods, and the Safonia Shipping Protection add-on.

## Key takeaways

- Free shipping on orders over $49 to the contiguous US; estimated 5–12 business days
- No cancellations or changes once a tracking code is generated; Beanz™ orders lock immediately at placement
- Address changes are impossible after an order is placed — no exceptions
- Refused delivery incurs a 20% restocking fee plus original shipping cost deducted from the refund
- Damaged, missing, or defective items must be reported within 5 business days of delivery
- Open Box items are Final Sale — no returns
