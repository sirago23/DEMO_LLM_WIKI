# Source: Refund Policy — Seattle Coffee Gear

**Original:** `raw/Refund policy _ Seattle Coffee Gear.pdf` | **Ingested:** 2026-08-08

## Summary

The full return and refund policy for Seattle Coffee Gear covering retail and online/phone purchases. Defines the 30-day return window, Open Box Final Sale rule, RMA process, item-type eligibility (machines, accessories, consumables, parts), restocking fees, and the "Like New" condition requirement. Also covers the 5-day delivery claims window (shared with the shipping policy) and an extended holiday return period.

## Key takeaways

- 30-day return window: retail from purchase date; online/phone from ship date
- Open Box items are Final Sale — not eligible for return
- RMA required for all returns; email support@seattlecoffeegear.com with order number
- Consumables are never eligible for return, even if sealed; cost auto-deducted from all returns
- 5% restocking fee for items over $1,000; shipping costs not reimbursed
- Self-installed PID or plumbing mods void return eligibility; factory/SCG-installed PIDs are eligible
- Miele machines require Miele authorization (1-800-999-1360) before RMA
- Replaced defective items get a new 5-day window or remainder of original window, whichever is longer
- Extended holiday window: purchases 11/1/2025–12/24/2025 eligible until 1/23/2026
