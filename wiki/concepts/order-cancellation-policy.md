# Order Cancellation and Changes Policy

Rules covering when and whether an order can be cancelled, modified, or redirected after placement.

## General rule

**Once a tracking code has been generated, Seattle Coffee Gear will not cancel or make changes to your order.** This includes item changes, quantity changes, and address changes.

## Beanz™ orders (stricter rule)

For orders containing coffee Powered by [[entities/beanz]] (identified by the "Powered by Beanz™" logo on the product page under "Product details"):
- Changes and cancellations are **not possible as soon as the order is placed** — before tracking is generated
- This covers address changes, item changes, cancellations, and recurring subscription orders
- A specific shipping method cannot be selected for Beanz™ items regardless of the rest of the order; contact SCG before ordering if a specific method is required

## Address changes

- Addresses are locked at the time of order placement — no exceptions
- SCG cannot change shipping addresses, redirect packages, or place holds on shipped packages
- **Verify your shipping address before purchasing**

## Refused delivery

If you refuse delivery or return items without first requesting an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization):
- A **20% restocking fee** is deducted from your refund
- **Original shipping charges** are also deducted from your refund
