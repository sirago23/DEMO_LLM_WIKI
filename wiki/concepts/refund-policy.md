# Refund Policy

Seattle Coffee Gear's rules governing exchanges, returns, and refunds for all purchase channels.

## Return windows

| Purchase channel | Window | Starts from |
|-----------------|--------|-------------|
| Retail store | 30 days | Date of purchase |
| Phone or website | 30 days | Ship date |
| Open Box items | Not eligible | Final Sale — no returns |

**Extended holiday window:** Purchases made in-store or online between 11/1/2025 and 12/24/2025 are eligible for return until 1/23/2026.

## Item eligibility

### Electric appliances (espresso machines, coffee makers, grinders, roasters, blenders)
- Must be returned in [[concepts/like-new-condition]]
- Refund = original purchase price minus original shipping & handling costs and any applicable restocking fees
- Equipment not in Like New condition will be assessed for damage; refurbishment fees may apply
- **Self-installed PID or plumbing adaptations:** not eligible for return
- **Factory-installed or SCG-installed PIDs:** still eligible
- **Miele machines:** contact Miele at 1-800-999-1360 for return authorization before requesting RMA

### Non-consumable accessories (non-electric)
- Must be new and unopened
- Refund = price paid minus any shipping & handling costs

### Free or included non-consumable accessories
- Full credit if returned unopened and unused with the original machine
- If exchanging, keep free accessories and receive new ones with the replacement machine

### Consumable items
- **Never eligible for return**, even if sealed (includes coffee, syrups, sauces, cleaning supplies, filters)
- Cost of consumables is automatically deducted from all returns

### Parts
- Not eligible for return

### Open Box items
- Final Sale — not eligible for return or exchange
- Open Box items are noted as Final Sale on their product page

## Restocking fees

- Items over $1,000: SCG reserves the right to charge a 5% restocking fee
- Some vendor-direct items may carry a separate restocking fee (disclosed during the return process)
- Retail: used/opened items charged 10%; damaged/dirty/missing-accessories items up to 20%

## Shipping costs

- Shipping and handling fees are not reimbursed
- If free shipping was selected at checkout, SCG deducts its original shipping & handling cost from the refund
- Customer is responsible for the cost of return shipping

## Promotional gift cards

- Promotional gift cards included with a purchase have no cash value
- On return: gift card balance is cancelled and any redeemed value is deducted from the final refund
