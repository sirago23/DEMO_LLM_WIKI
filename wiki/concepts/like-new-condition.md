# Like New Condition

The condition standard required for returning electric appliances (espresso machines, coffee makers, grinders, etc.) to Seattle Coffee Gear.

## Definition

SCG understands a machine used a few times will not be absolutely perfect, but it must be close enough to resell as a certified used machine.

### All original components intact
Return every original component: original packaging, drip tray, portafilter, brew head (if removable), filter screens, filter baskets, user manuals, tampers, CDs, scoops, and any other item that came in the box.

### No scratches, dents, or stains
The machine and all components must be free of any surface marring.

### Thoroughly cleaned
The machine, all parts, and components must be completely clean — free of coffee grounds, water, and milk residues.

## What happens if a machine does not meet this standard

Equipment not received in Like New condition will be assessed for damage and may be subject to refurbishment fees.
