# Damaged, Missing, and Defective Merchandise Policy

How to report and resolve issues with damaged, missing, or defective items from Seattle Coffee Gear.

## Reporting window

**Report all issues within 5 business days of delivery.** Issues reported after this window may not be eligible for resolution.

## Damaged merchandise

- Items are carefully packaged to protect against shipping damage
- Inspect your package immediately upon arrival
- If an item appears damaged: **do not attempt to use it** — notify SCG immediately to begin the claims process

## Missing merchandise

- SCG often ships orders in multiple packages — check whether a second package is in transit before reporting
- If items are genuinely missing, report immediately so SCG can track packages and issue a replacement

## Defective or malfunctioning new merchandise

- Contact SCG immediately if an item is not working or a consumable arrived expired
- SCG will help set up a replacement and assist with troubleshooting
- Have documentation of the defect ready to speed up the return/replacement process

## Normal conditions (not defects)

- Grinders may have coffee grounds or residue — this is expected for tested units
- Espresso machines may have a small amount of water or ground coffee — also expected
- If concerned, contact support@seattlecoffeegear.com with photos

## Contact

- General support: support@seattlecoffeegear.com
