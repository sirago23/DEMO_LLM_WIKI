# Shipping Policy

Seattle Coffee Gear's shipping rules covering geographic coverage, free and paid shipping terms, carrier selection, and special item categories.

## Geographic coverage

- Ships to all 50 US states
- APO/military addresses: some items accepted, weight restrictions may apply, must order by phone (not online)
- **No international shipping**

## Free shipping

- Available on orders over **$49** to the **contiguous US only**
- Excludes Alaska, Hawaii, APO/military addresses, and international orders
- Delivery estimate: **5–12 business days** (carrier estimate, not guaranteed)
- SCG uses the lowest-cost carrier for free shipping orders
- You may upgrade to a faster paid method at checkout

## Paid shipping

- **Utah Distribution Center cutoffs (ground carrier):** order before 4pm Mountain Time → ships same business day; weekend orders ship next business day
- **Utah Distribution Center cutoffs (air carrier):** order before 1pm Mountain Time → ships same business day; weekend orders ship next business day
- Paid shipping is guaranteed up to the amount paid; if the date is missed, contact Customer Support for a shipping charge refund

## Carrier and shipping method

- SCG reserves the right to use a different carrier than selected at checkout, provided the service level is comparable
- SCG cannot ship on a customer's personal shipping account

## Tracking

- Tracking numbers are posted at shipment or as soon as available
- Backordered items: SCG typically holds the entire order until the backordered item is ready, then ships together
- Signature requirements cannot be removed for any reason

## Bulk order limits

- Filters, cleaning products, and select parts: maximum 10 per customer
- Larger quantities or resale: contact inside.sales@seattlecoffeegear.com

## Commercial equipment and freight

- Customer is responsible for all freight charges on commercial machine shipments
- Alaska and Hawaii freight charges may exceed continental US rates; SCG will contact you before shipping if discrepancies arise

## Free/bonus items

- Free or bonus items may be substituted with items of similar value and shipped via the lowest-cost method

## Open Box equipment

- Open Box items may be missing non-essential accessories, filters, or cleaning supplies
- Open Box items include the original Manufacturer's Warranty
- **Open Box items are Final Sale — not eligible for return**

## Payment methods accepted

Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Amazon Pay, Apple Pay, Shop Pay, Seattle Coffee Gear Gift Cards/Gift Certificates, Affirm
